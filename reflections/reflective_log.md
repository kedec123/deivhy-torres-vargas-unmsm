# Reflective Log Template

This file is intentionally a template, not a completed reflection. The author should write the entries in the first person after each course unit. The course guide asks for approximately 2,000-3,000 words in total, built over time rather than reconstructed at the end. AI must not generate the reflective content; ordinary grammar checking may be used only if disclosed under the project policy.

Use the cycle **What happened? -> So what changed in my thinking? -> Now what will I do differently?** Each entry should name a real decision, evidence, disagreement, error, or unresolved tension and connect it to a repository artefact.

## Entry 1: Unit I (Sessions 1-4) - Paradigm, method, and literature

**What happened?** What initial assumption did I make about anemia, the research question, or the method? Which part of `01_paradigm/`, `02_method/`, or `04_literature/` challenged it?

**So what?** What changed in my understanding of a quantitative association, a causal claim, or the 2024 comparability issue? What tension remains unresolved?

**Now what?** What did I change in `03_protocol/` because of that learning?

## Entry 2: Unit II (Sessions 5-7) - Reproducibility

**What happened?** What broke, surprised me, or required a different decision while building the data pipeline, DVC setup, MLflow record, or reproducibility audit?

**So what?** Which assumption about "running code" versus "reproducing a result" did this challenge?

**Now what?** Which file, command, test, or documentation change did I make? What still needs external verification?

## Entry 3: Unit III (Sessions 9-12) - Ethics, data, bias, and integrity

**What happened?** Which risk, fairness trade-off, data-management decision, or retraction lesson was difficult to resolve?

**So what?** How did it change the way I read a metric, a subgroup difference, or an AI-assisted text?

**Now what?** What boundary did I add to the model card, ethics protocol, DMP, or AI policy? What decision do I still need to revisit?

## Entry 4: Unit IV (Sessions 13-15) - Writing and peer review

**What happened?** What feedback did I receive or give? Which part was hardest to accept or defend?

**So what?** Did the feedback expose a gap between v0.1, v1.0, and the planned v2.0?

**Now what?** Which specific revision did I accept, reject, or defer, and why? Link it to the v2.0 response table.

## Final synthesis (Session 16)

What is the most important change in how I approach doctoral research after this course? What will I carry into the next stage of this anemia study, and what remains provisional? Connect the answer to at least two concrete repository decisions.
