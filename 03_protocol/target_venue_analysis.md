# Target Venue Analysis

## Provisional target

The current first-choice venue is the *Revista Peruana de Medicina Experimental y Salud Pública* (RPMESP). This is a planning decision, not a submission. The journal is the scientific publication of Peru's National Institute of Health and its author instructions describe a peer-reviewed outlet for biomedical and public-health contributions with practical relevance to Peru and the region. Its scope therefore fits a transparent ENDES analysis that is written as population health evidence rather than as a clinical AI product.

## Fit and conditions

| Criterion | Current assessment | Consequence for this project |
|---|---|---|
| Topic and setting | Strong fit: child anemia and ENDES are Peruvian public-health topics. | Keep the question anchored in a policy-relevant population description. |
| Design | Potential fit: secondary cross-sectional analysis is familiar to the literature reviewed. | Complete design-based uncertainty and report the study design clearly. |
| Language | The checked instructions state that submissions are written in Spanish. | Translate and author-review any future manuscript; the course repository may remain in English. |
| Reporting | The final paper will need a complete methods and results record, not only a course pipeline. | Use STROBE and the final protocol checklist before submission. |
| Data governance | Public-use source material still has access and reuse conditions. | Confirm INEI conditions and institutional requirements before public data release. |
| Reproducibility | The repository provides a strong starting record but the shared DVC transfer is not yet verified. | Do not claim a stranger test until the service-account remote and clean-clone pull pass. |

## Decision boundary

RPMESP is appropriate only if the final study remains a carefully reported Peruvian public-health analysis and the author completes the pending survey-design, access, and manuscript-language requirements. If the work shifts toward an international methods paper, a different venue should be assessed at that time rather than forcing the study to fit this preliminary choice.
