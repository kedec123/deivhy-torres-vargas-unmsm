# Included Studies

| ID | Citation and persistent link | Design and population | Contribution to this project |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Velasquez-Hurtado et al. (2016), *Biomedica*, 36(2), 220-229. [PMID 27622483](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27622483/) | National ENDES secondary analysis; children 6-35 months, 2007-2013. | Core evidence on observed socioeconomic, child, and maternal-care associations. |
| P02 | Campos-Sanchez et al. (2025), *Revista Peruana de Medicina Experimental y Salud Publica*, 42(2), 115-125. [doi:10.17843/rpmesp.2025.422.14028](https://doi.org/10.17843/rpmesp.2025.422.14028) | Weighted ENDES analysis; children 6-35 months, 2009-2023. | Shows why a 2024 definition change cannot be silently combined with the historical series. |
| P03 | Westgard et al. (2021), *BMC Nutrition*, 7, 11. [doi:10.1186/s40795-021-00417-3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40795-021-00417-3) | Cross-sectional analysis; 586 children aged 6-35 months. | Adds food, infection, and safe-water context; does not establish causation. |
| P04 | Munayco et al. (2013), *Revista Peruana de Medicina Experimental y Salud Publica*, 30(2), 229-234. [PMID 23949507](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23949507/) | Sentinel cohort in three Andean regions; children 6-35 months. | Illustrates programme monitoring and its context-specific character. |
| P05 | Huaman-Espino et al. (2012), *Revista Peruana de Medicina Experimental y Salud Publica*, 29(3), 314-323. [doi:10.1590/s1726-46342012000300004](https://doi.org/10.1590/s1726-46342012000300004) | Multistage cross-sectional study in Apurimac; children 6-35 months. | Shows that supplement receipt and adequate consumption are distinct implementation questions. |
| P06 | Choque-Quispe et al. (2020), *Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences*, 1473(1), 35-47. [doi:10.1111/nyas.14356](https://doi.org/10.1111/nyas.14356) | Observational study in high-altitude Peru. | Supports cautious interpretation of altitude adjustment and prevalence. |
| P07 | Creed-Kanashiro et al. (2016), *Maternal & Child Nutrition*, 12(1), 152-163. [doi:10.1111/mcn.12217](https://doi.org/10.1111/mcn.12217) | Interviews and observations with caregivers and health personnel. | Supplies implementation context that the present quantitative design cannot capture. |
| P08 | Sobrino et al. (2014), *Revista Panamericana de Salud Publica*, 35(2), 104-112. [PMID 24781091](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24781091/) | National ENDES trend and determinant analysis in children under five. | Historical benchmark for social and territorial patterning. |
| P09 | Tarazona-Meza et al. (2025), *Global Health Action*, 18(1), 2475580. [doi:10.1080/16549716.2025.2475580](https://doi.org/10.1080/16549716.2025.2475580) | Qualitative implementation study in urban Cuna Mas centres. | Keeps policy interpretation grounded in delivery barriers and facilitators. |
| S01 | Castro-Mayta (2023), *Revista de la Facultad de Medicina Humana*, 23(4), 41-53. [doi:10.25176/rfmh.v23i4.5497](https://doi.org/10.25176/rfmh.v23i4.5497) | Cross-sectional secondary analysis of ENDES 2021. | Demonstrates the care needed when interpreting service-use indicators and anemia prevalence. |

The included literature was used to frame the question, variables, comparability issue, and interpretation boundary. It was not used to manufacture expected results for the ENDES analysis.
