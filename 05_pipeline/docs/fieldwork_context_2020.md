# ENDES 2020 fieldwork context

The ENDES 2020 point estimate is retained because the survey remains part of the specified time series, but it is interpreted with explicit collection-context caution. The INEI technical sheet records that the COVID-19 national emergency required adaptations to the survey operation: collection was suspended during the mandatory isolation period, telephone interviews were implemented, and in-person collection resumed gradually with biosecurity measures.

This context does not invalidate the estimate and does not demonstrate bias by itself. It means that the lower 2020 point estimate and smaller eligible analytical sample should not be presented as definitive evidence of a real short-term change in anemia burden without acknowledging the altered collection conditions.

Source: Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática. [Ficha Técnica — ENDES 2020](https://www.inei.gob.pe/descargar/encuestas/documentos/ficha-tecnica-endes-2020.pdf).
