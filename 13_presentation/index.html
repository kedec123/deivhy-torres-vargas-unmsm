Research question and boundary

Population description and associations — not causal or clinical prediction.

Question

How did legacy-comparable anemia prevalence change from 2019 to 2024, and which measured characteristics were associated with anemia?

Data and design

57,539 de-identified, tabular ENDES records. Numeric and categorical variables; ENDES weights, strata and primary sampling units are retained for inference.

The separate machine-learning exercise is a reproducible binary-classification teaching artefact. It does not diagnose, triage, rank or treat children.

Interpretation safeguards

Three safeguards prevent an over-read of the evidence.

2024 definition

HW57 remains primary for comparability. The updated HW57A estimate (34.9%) is reported only as a measurement sensitivity, not mixed into the trend.

2020 conditions

INEI adapted collection during the COVID-19 emergency, including telephone interviews and gradual return to fieldwork. The 2020 dip is not treated as a decisive health change.

Classifier

Three models × five prespecified 80/20 splits = 15 local runs. Internal AUC near 0.71 is not epidemiological, external or clinical performance.

Descriptive and associative claims only6 / 7
What this project delivers

A transparent ENDES workflow that can be checked, rebuilt and extended.

  • Public-source retrieval with checksum verification and a documented de-identification route.
  • Design-based annual uncertainty plus bootstrap comparison.
  • Explicit measurement sensitivity, adjusted association boundaries and no clinical claim.
