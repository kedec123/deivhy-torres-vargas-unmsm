UNMSM · Study frame
Research question

What can these data describe — and what can they not establish?

How did comparable child-anemia prevalence change from 2019 to 2024, and which observed characteristics were associated with anemia?

Claim boundary

The study describes population patterns and adjusted associations. It does not establish causal effects or support diagnosis, triage, or treatment.

UNMSM · Interpretation safeguards

Interpretation boundaries

Three checks keep the comparison interpretable.

01

A consistent primary definition

HW57 is the primary outcome in every year. The updated 2024 HW57A estimate (34.9%) is reported only as a sensitivity check and is not combined with the historical series.

02

2020 needs context

During the COVID-19 emergency, INEI adapted collection through telephone interviews and a gradual return to fieldwork. The 2020 dip is therefore not treated as a decisive change in child health.

03

The claims remain limited

Observed associations are not causal effects. The data do not support clinical decisions, child ranking or medical recommendations.

2020 context documented from INEI technical information06 / 09
This slide demonstrates methodological maturity. Emphasise why the 2024 measurement change and 2020 fieldwork context make simple raw comparisons potentially misleading.
